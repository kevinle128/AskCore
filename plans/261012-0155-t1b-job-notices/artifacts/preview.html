Background job notices TUI design preview (draft)

Each box is one terminal at the stated size. Rows above the dashed line are native scrollback; rows below it are the live region that ask redraws. The dashed line is not on screen. Colors follow docs/tui-style-guide.md. Source: artifacts/sketch-gen.py, the same rows as design.md. All commands and job ids are examples.
