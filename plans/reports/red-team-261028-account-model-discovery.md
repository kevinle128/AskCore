## Rejected

| Finding | Reason |
|---|---|
| Scope the cache by fingerprint (Failure 3) | The cache holds the raw models.dev payload and plain id lists; each binary maps them with its own rules. A fingerprint protects nothing and would drop account lists after each upgrade (Scope 4 is right). Kept: schema version; an older binary does not overwrite a newer schema; a write keeps the newer `fetchedAt`. |
| Replace the hand-written rows with id patches in `catalog_rules.go` (Scope 5) | The 6 rows have cited sources and many tests. Family rules hold family defaults only; per-id data stays in the hand-written rows. One mechanism per level, not two for one job. |
